# Kavach for Societies: Problem Evidence

As of 2026-10-08

## Overview

Indian apartment societies face repeated, documented emergencies around lifts, power backup, water and fire, and no one tracks them in real time. Existing society apps (MyGate, NoBrokerHood) handle visitors and maintenance bills, not emergency dispatch.

## Laws and regulation

Lift laws exist, but day-to-day compliance is not tracked, and the Supreme Court now holds building owners jointly liable. A timestamped incident log doubles as a compliance record.

| Law or ruling | What it requires | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supreme Court ruling (29 July) | Lifts are "common carriers"; manufacturer, maintenance agency and building owner jointly responsible for passenger safety | [Deccan Herald](https://deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/four-in-10-bengalureans-dread-stepping-into-a-lift-survey-4098155) |
| Tamil Nadu Lifts Act, 1997 | Permission to install and a licence to operate every lift; government inspectors | [Casemine](https://www.casemine.com/act/in/5a9ccdfd4a93265347813113) |
| Karnataka Lifts, Escalators and Passenger Conveyors Act | Registration of installers and maintainers; inspectors may enter and inspect | [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7182/1/9_of_2013_%28e%29.pdf), [NoBrokerHood](https://www.nobrokerhood.com/blog/karnataka-lifts-escalators-and-passenger-conveyors-act/) |
| UP Lifts and Escalators Act, 2024 | Registration, annual testing, automatic rescue devices, uploaded maintenance certificates; late-2024 report said none of Noida's ~80,000 high-rise lifts were registered | [Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/trending/lift-and-escalator-act-2024-noida-dominates-registration-drive-across-uttar-pradesh), [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19956/1/act_lift_eng.pdf) |
| Karnataka fire rules | High-rises renew fire NOC every 2 years; non-compliance can lead to power disconnection | [Citizen Matters](https://citizenmatters.in/articles/3358-new-fire-safety-norms-for-bengaluru-high-rises) |

## Five Problems slide

Each problem on the slide maps to a feature Kavach already has or will get during the rebrand.

| Problem | Evidence to cite | Kavach feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lift entrapment | 31% of Bengalureans trapped in a past year; KP Park death | Lift SOS with priority scoring and automatic escalation |
| Backup failures nobody tracks | KP Park: generator broken 2.5 years, lift battery dead | Asset status and backup-power tiers (P1 lifts and pumps first) |
| Water outages | Bengaluru 2024 rationing and doubled tanker prices | New water incident type; pumps as P1 zones |
| Floods and network failure | Michaung left Chennai apartments cut off for days | Offline-first app with a queued SOS that sends when the network returns |
| Fire and compliance gaps | Vadapalani basement fire; 149 Chennai buildings flagged | Fire incidents plus a timestamped audit log for the residents' association |
